# SOP-PR-045 Line Clearance

SOP: SOP-PR-045 · Version: 2.2 · Status: effective · Effective from: 2026-02-01

## When line clearance is needed
Before every new batch, after maintenance and after cleaning, the line is cleared so that no material, labels or documents from the previous batch remain.

## Steps
The operator removes all remaining vials, components, labels and printed material, cleans the line according to the cleaning plan and checks the checklist in the batch record. A second person verifies the clearance and signs. Production starts only after both signatures.
