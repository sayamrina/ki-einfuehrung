# SOP-QA-012 Deviation Management

SOP: SOP-QA-012 · Version: 4.0 · Status: effective · Effective from: 2026-05-01

## Reporting a deviation
Any unplanned departure from an approved procedure, specification or batch instruction is a deviation. The person who notices it records it in the quality management system within 24 hours and informs the shift supervisor at once. Affected material is labelled "on hold" and kept apart until QA decides.

## Classification
QA classifies every deviation as minor, major or critical, based on the possible impact on product quality and patient safety. Production may propose a class, but only QA sets it.

## Investigation and CAPA
Major and critical deviations need a root cause investigation, completed within 30 calendar days. Corrective and preventive actions (CAPA) are defined, assigned to an owner and tracked to closure. Only QA closes a deviation.
