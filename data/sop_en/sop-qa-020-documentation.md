# SOP-QA-020 Good Documentation Practice

SOP: SOP-QA-020 · Version: 5.1 · Status: effective · Effective from: 2026-01-15

## Principles
GxP records must be attributable, legible, contemporaneous, original and accurate (ALCOA+), as well as complete, consistent, enduring and available. Entries are made at the time the work is done, by the person who did it.

## Corrections
A wrong entry is crossed out with a single line so that it stays readable, and the correct value is written next to it with the date, initials and a reason. Correction fluid, overwriting and pencil are not allowed. Entries are never backdated.

## Electronic records
In electronic systems every change is captured in the audit trail with user, time, old and new value and reason. Shared accounts are not allowed.
