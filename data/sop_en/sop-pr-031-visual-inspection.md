# SOP-PR-031 Visual Inspection of Vials

SOP: SOP-PR-031 · Version: 3.0 · Status: effective · Effective from: 2026-06-15

## Purpose and scope
Describes the manual visual inspection of glass vials on packaging lines 1 to 3 before they are packed.

## Defect classes
Critical defects are cracks, missing or broken parts and foreign particles inside the vial. Major defects are scratches in the sealing area and wrong dimensions. Minor defects are cosmetic marks outside functional areas.

## Sampling
Each lot is sampled according to the sampling plan with an AQL of 0.065 for critical defects and 0.65 for major defects. Inspectors work in a booth with 2,000 to 3,750 lux and take a break of at least 10 minutes every hour.

## Rejected vials
Rejected vials go into the red container, are counted and recorded in the batch record. If the number of critical defects exceeds the action limit, the line is stopped and a deviation is reported under SOP-QA-012.
