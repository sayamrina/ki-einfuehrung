# Fuel and Service Cards

Area: Customer service · Updated: 09/2026

## Blocking a card
Lost or stolen cards are blocked immediately via the service hotline or in the customer portal. The block applies from the moment it is reported. The member company is not liable for transactions after the block.

## Replacement card
A replacement card is sent by post to the registered company address within five working days. For security reasons, the PIN arrives in a separate letter.

## Billing
Refuelling is billed twice a month, on the 15th and at the end of the month. Customer service answers questions about individual transactions; objections are possible within 30 days of the invoice date.
