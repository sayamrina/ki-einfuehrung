# Training for Professional Drivers

Area: Training · Updated: 07/2026

## Training obligation
Professional drivers must complete 35 hours of training every five years. It consists of five modules of seven hours each.

## Registration
Member companies register their drivers via the customer portal. Free cancellation is possible up to ten days before the course starts.

## Proof
After each module, participants receive a certificate of attendance. They apply for the code 95 entry in their driving licence themselves at the licensing authority.
