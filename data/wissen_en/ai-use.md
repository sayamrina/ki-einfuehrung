# Rules for Using AI Applications

Area: Data protection and IT · Updated: 10/2026

## Permitted applications
Only approved AI applications may be used for company data. Private accounts with AI services are not allowed for company data.

## Personal data
Names, addresses, licence plates linked to people, bank details and health data may only be entered into applications that pseudonymise this data automatically or are explicitly approved for it.

## Checking results
AI results are drafts. Before anything is sent to customers, members or authorities, a person always checks the result. Errors are reported to the AI team.
