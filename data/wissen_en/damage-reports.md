# Damage Reports in Motor Insurance

Area: Insurance · Updated: 08/2026

## What a report must contain
Licence plate, date and place of the damage, a short description of what happened, photos and, for accidents with other parties, their details and, if applicable, the police file number.

## Deadlines
Damage should be reported within one week. Injuries must be reported immediately.

## Process
Case handlers check completeness, request missing documents and assign a claim number. For damage above 10,000 euros, an expert report is usually commissioned.
